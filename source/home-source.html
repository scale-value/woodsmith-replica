What maintenance is required for a deck or patio?

Maintenance depends on the material. Composite and PVC decking generally require routine cleaning, while wood decking may require periodic staining or sealing. Patio maintenance varies by material and joint system but is typically minimal.

Can you build decks & patios year-round?

Yes, we build decks & patios throughout the year, though weather conditions may impact the timeline during certain seasons.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. When permits and inspections are required as part of our scope, we coordinate the applicable submissions and inspections with the local municipality.

What materials do you use to build decks?

We work with a variety of materials, including wood, composite, and PVC, depending on your preferences and budget.

How long does a typical project take?

Project duration depends on size, complexity, permitting, inspections, material availability, and weather. We provide a more specific schedule once the scope and project details are established.

Do you offer custom deck & patio designs?

Yes, we create custom deck & patio designs tailored to your space, style, and specific needs.
