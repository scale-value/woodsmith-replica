Flex Screen

Discover excellence in Porch Enclosure Systems, featuring the exclusive FlexScreen®. Crafted from flexible spring steel coated in exterior-grade PVC, FlexScreen® is virtually indestructible, weather-resistant, and scratch-proof. Its tension-held frame seamlessly disappears into the screen pocket, providing an unobstructed view. Quick installation and removal make cleaning a breeze. Elevate your experience with the hassle-free brilliance of FlexScreen — an integral part of our acclaimed SummerSpace Porch Enclosure Systems..

Trim Kit

Enhance your porch with our two-piece aluminum powder-coated trim — a seamless addition for a polished look. Choose from two sizes catering to different gap widths. This unique design attaches effortlessly, hiding screws with a snap-on cap. With a panel-matching low gloss finish, it's versatile for indoor and outdoor use. For a flawless weatherproof seal, explore our Geocell Phantom translucent sealant. And for those rare scratches, our touch-up paint bottles and slim pens have you covered..

East to Remove Sashes

Experience the freedom of custom comfort with Porch Enclosure System on warm, breezy days. Transform your porch into a refreshing screen enclosure effortlessly thanks to our user-friendly window pane design. With minimal effort, you can remove and reinsert the panes at will, adapting to changing weather with ease. Removing the panes is as simple as lifting them upwards and sliding them out from the bottom. To restore, lift upward, guide the pane into the groove, and allow it to slide down into place. Revel in the flexibility of tailor-made relaxation as you embrace the outdoors on your own terms..
